Georgian Balance, Classical Proportion & Restraint

Georgian rooms rely on classical symmetry, tall sash windows and disciplined hierarchy between skirting, dado rail, fielded wall panels and cornice. New cabinetry should echo the vertical rhythm of the window reveals rather than fighting the room’s height.

Profiles & Proportions Quarter-round ovolo mouldings, raised and fielded panels, 1:1.618 Golden Ratio dado-to-frieze division, fine cockbeaded in-frame doors.
Timber, Paint & Colour Suggestions Painted tulipwood in warm stone, chalky off-whites, drab olive or deep library greens; aged unlacquered brass butt hinges and knobs.
Historical Evidence vs. Contemporary Interpretation Rather than pastiche reproduction, we use authentic Georgian stile-and-rail widths to conceal modern media systems, acoustic panelling and dressing storage.
