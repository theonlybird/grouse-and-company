Clear Project Control · Defined Sequence

A clear route from approval to installation.

Each stage is completed and signed off before the next begins, linking design approvals, workshop production and staged payments (`50% / 40% / 10%`) to clear milestones.

Stage 1 Payment · 50% 1

Agreement & Deposit

Once your proposal scope is approved and the deposit is received, your project is scheduled into our programme and moves into technical survey.

2

Technical Site Measure

Final laser dimensions are taken on site, confirming out-of-square wall tolerances, ceiling levels, skirting junctions, service routes and delivery access.

3

Design & Specification Sign-Off

Timber species, paint colours, Armac Martin brass hardware, internal accessories and moulding profiles are finalised with physical sample boards.

4

CAD Drawings & Production Pack

Detailed technical drawings and production information are prepared for client or architect sign-off before anything is released to the workshop floor.

Stage 2 Payment · 40% 5

Production, Assembly & QC

Joinery is crafted at The Workshop, quality-checked, spray-finished or hand-oiled, and where needed pre-assembled and test-fitted prior to dispatch.

Final Balance · 10% 6

Installation, Handover & Aftercare

Installed with full floor and room protection, final hinge adjustments, snag closure, and our wax-sealed Care Booklet & Finish Schedule.
